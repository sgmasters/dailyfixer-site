Optional motivation, with no penalties. Settings → Gamification: **Full**, **Light** or **Off**.

## XP

| Event | XP |
|---|---|
| Task completed (no priority) | 10 |
| Task completed with priority: 10 + 5 per level (P1 urgent = 25) | 15–25 |
| Sub-task completed | 3 |
| Mission completed | the mission's value |
| Task reopened | the XP it earned is taken back |

## Levels
Level *n* is reached at 50 · n · (n − 1) XP.

| Level | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total XP | 100 | 300 | 600 | 1,000 | 1,500 | 4,500 |

## Streaks
A day counts when you complete at least one task. Your streak lasts until the end of today.

## Missions
Missions are made for each day, week and month and expire at the end of it; completed missions stay completed. N is based on your last two weeks: the average per day, rounded up, plus 1, kept between 2 and 8.

| Period | Missions |
|---|---|
| Daily | Complete N tasks · Finish one important task (P2 or higher) · One step toward an active goal |
| Weekly | Complete 5N tasks · Three steps toward a goal (or five important tasks) |
| Monthly | Complete 20N tasks |

Badges and unlockable color themes are on the **Progress** screen.
