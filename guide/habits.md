From version **0.5**, DailyFixer tracks habits: things you want to do regularly, like stretching, drinking water or reading.

## Habit or repeating task?
| | Habit | Repeating task |
|---|---|---|
| A missed day | leaves nothing behind; only that habit's streak starts again | stays as an overdue task |
| Kinds | Yes/No ("Stretch") or a count per day ("8 glasses of water") | done / not done |
| Best for | daily routines | things that must not be forgotten: bills, appointments |

## Create a habit
Missions → **Habits** → **New habit**:

| Field | Options |
|---|---|
| Name | what you want to do |
| Kind | **Yes/No**, or **Count** with a daily target and a unit ("8 glasses") |
| How often | **Every day** · **On days** (pick weekdays) · **Per week** (any 1–7 days of a Monday–Sunday week) |
| Reminder | optional time; as a notification, or through Telegram when your bot is set up |

## Check in
- **Today** shows the habits planned for today. Tap a Yes/No habit to mark it done (tap again to undo). Tap a count habit to add 1 ("3/8 glasses").
- Long press for more: +1 / −1, mark done, **Skip**, clear, open.
- From a reminder: **Done** checks in (adds 1 for a count habit), **Snooze** reminds you again in an hour.
- By voice or quick add: "did yoga", "drank 2 glasses of water", "check in reading". Russian and Bulgarian work too ("сделал зарядку", "направих йога").
- Forgot yesterday? Open the habit and tap the day in the grid.

## Streaks and rewards
| Item | How it works |
|---|---|
| Streak | days in a row the habit was done, counting only its planned days. Today never breaks it while it is still open. Weekly habits count weeks |
| Skip | a day off (ill, travelling): the streak continues, but the day isn't counted as done |
| 30-day rate | how often you kept the habit in the last 30 days |
| XP | +5 when a habit reaches its target for the day; undo takes it back. No XP for a skip |
| Mission | on days with habits: "Check in N habits" (+20 XP) |
| Badges | 7-day and 30-day habit streaks, 100 check-ins |

No penalties: a missed day only restarts that habit's streak.

## New habit by voice
"new habit: read 20 min every weekday at 21:00", "add a habit gym 3 times a week at 7am", "new habit drink 8 glasses a day". DailyFixer shows what it understood; confirm to create it.

## Edit, archive, delete
Open a habit → **Edit**, **Archive** (off Today, no reminders; restore any time from Archived) or **Delete** (Undo right after). Habits are synced and backed up like your tasks.
