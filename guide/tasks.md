## Today
Four sections:
- **Overdue**, with *Move to today* (undoable)
- **Schedule**: tasks with a time, plus events from connected calendars
- **Anytime**: tasks due today without a time
- **Completed today**

## Tasks tab
- Filters: All, No date, Upcoming, Overdue, P1.
- Groups: Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later, No date. Completed tasks can be shown or hidden.
- **On a row**: tap to open; tick the checkbox or swipe right to complete; swipe left to delete. Both show *Undo*.

## Task details
Changes save automatically. You can set:
- priority: P1 urgent, P2 high, P3 medium, or none
- due date with an optional time
- reminders, notes and tags
- sub-tasks, added inline
- repeat

**History** shows every change to the task.

- **Date and time**: the due sheet has **All day** and **Pick time**. When you reschedule a task that has a time (by swipe, the row menu or several selected tasks), **Keep time** keeps it.
- **Sub-tasks**: completing a task also completes its open sub-tasks. Reopening a task affects only that task. Up to three levels are shown.
- **Repeat**: daily, weekly on chosen days, monthly on a day, every N days or weeks; ends on a date or after N times. Completing a repeating task creates the next one.

## Trash
Deleted tasks go to Trash. There you can restore them, delete them permanently, or empty Trash.
