**DailyFixer** is a free daily planner: tasks and sub-tasks, reminders, calendars, voice input, missions and learning packs. No ads, no tracking.

- **Local-first.** Everything is stored on your device and works offline, without an account.
- **Your cloud, encrypted.** Optional sync through your own Google Drive app folder, end-to-end encrypted with your passphrase. There is no DailyFixer server.
- **Platforms.** Android first (signed APK on GitHub Releases); iOS, Windows, Linux and macOS later.
